How to Play

Create a room and share its code with a friend. Both players receive the same grid and have five minutes to discover English words.

Drag or swipe horizontally, vertically, or diagonally, including backwards. Each valid word earns 10 points per letter. Both players can independently score the same word, but each player scores it only once.
